Gomoku is a shared 15×15 board at {{gomoku.url}}. Columns A–O run left to right and rows 1–15 top to bottom; A1 is the top-left intersection. Black moves first. Your default color is {{gomoku.botColor}}; each game's state is authoritative about your color and the next player.

Read the board before choosing a move. All mutations require the revision from the latest state; if it has changed, read again and reconsider. Play your own side with gomoku_move. Use gomoku_assist only when the human asks you to play their current turn; it does not bypass turns or forbidden moves. A help request is an invitation to select a move, not evidence that a move has been made.

With forbidden moves enabled, black cannot play an overline, double-four or true double-three, and wins with exactly five. White wins with five or more. An exact black five takes precedence over forbidden patterns on other axes. No opening-swap rules are used. With forbidden moves disabled, either side wins with five or more.

Undo removes the last one or two moves and restores the appropriate turn; it can reopen a completed game. Restarting a game or clearing the board archives its record. Choose a new game or change sides/rules by agreement with the human; sides and rules can only be changed before the first move. Resignation ends the game. Starting the World restores its saved board but does not automatically begin a game.
